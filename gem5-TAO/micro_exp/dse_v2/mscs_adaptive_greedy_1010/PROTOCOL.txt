Adaptive allocation of two candidate queries: frozen protocol, seed 42

Purpose and scope
  Test a single search change: does previous verified ranking quality tell us
  when to give both proposal slots to scalar or to MSCS? This is not lookahead,
  retraining, a new CPI model, or a test of Protect/Decouple mechanisms.
  Six previously inspected roots from the hybrid pilot. These are development
  cases, NOT held-out confirmation. No root selection or per-benchmark rules.

Unchanged numerical and search controls
  Same checkpoint, complete endpoint feature reconstruction, component-logit
  MSCS predictor and scalar-excess predictor as the hybrid pilot. Adaptive uses
  12 backward outputs and recovers scalar slopes from that component Jacobian.
  Same 15-parameter single-action generator, fixed width/L1 trace/BP, 6 rounds,
  two unseen candidate verifications per round, area cap 1.10 times root area.
  Both candidates are frozen before either is evaluated. Accept the feasible
  strict CPI improvement with lowest CPI; accept -> radius4, reject -> halve to1.

One fixed allocation rule; no tuning sweep
  Each trajectory starts with zero ranking losses. Lower cumulative ranking
  loss gets both slots. Equal loss uses the prior hybrid rule (scalar best,
  plus MSCS best distinct). Stable ties preserve generator order.
  AFTER both candidates have been evaluated, score both predictors on the
  same three pairs among {root, candidate1, candidate2}. The root's known CPI
  is the root prediction for both experts. Actual ties within 1e-6*root_CPI are
  omitted; a correct predicted order costs0, a predicted tie costs0.5, an
  inversion costs1. Store twice this loss as integers to avoid accumulator ties
  from rounding. The same tolerance defines predicted ties. Add losses over
  all completed rounds; no decay, learned threshold, prior or benchmark rules.
  Update affects NEXT round only. Neither unqueried labels, future decisions,
  other trajectories' feedback, area outcomes nor parameter names enter it.
  Area-infeasible candidates still have usable CPI labels; feasibility remains
  an independent exact area check. This is selected-candidate online feedback,
  not an unbiased whole-pool accuracy estimate or confidence certificate.
  Ranking, not absolute calibration: exaggerated but correct ordering is not
  penalized more than a small numerical error that preserves the same order.

Efficient controls and budget
  Reuse scalar_excess, mscs_logit and fixed hybrid trajectories read-only from
  mscs_hybrid_greedy_1010/results_s42_01. Independently replay that entire pilot
  before running. Freeze its source/result hashes and checkpoint identity.
  Do NOT use those baseline outcomes to initialize/update this allocator.
  Run only adaptive: at most 6*6*2 = 72 NEW candidate forward calls; no extra
  oracle audit, new gem5, retraining, extra seeds or post-result rule changes.
  Source-salted exact area caches may be read; new areas use a private store.
  Record physical and logical costs separately. Historical wall times are not
  a controlled runtime comparison; do not claim an end-to-end speedup.

Checks and reporting, fixed before outcomes
  Tests cover timing/causality, ties/dedup/budget, expert-swap symmetry after
  informative feedback, ranking versus calibration, and reversible leadership.
  Independent saved-trace replay checks every prediction, frozen selection,
  state update, candidate legality, acceptance, area constraints and budget.
  Report final CPI gain, all even budgets2/4/6/8/10/12, first budget reaching
  scalar's final CPI, expert allocation counts, and informative comparisons.
  Show all six cases, including losses/ties. Explain divergence from fixed
  hybrid using verified candidates only. Report area failures and whether their
  missing values could change acceptance. Preserve every old file and result.
