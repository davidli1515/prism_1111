What the automatic-pair experiments are testing

The fixed model predicts stage/slice probabilities (Base,FP,BP,SP). Let
ell_k = log(P_k/Base), q_k = exp(ell_k)/sum_j exp(ell_j). At a fixed width W,
ignoring the explicit safety clamps for the derivation only,

    C(x) = [1 + sum_k exp(ell_k(x))] / W.

For a finite action i with COMPLETE encoded displacement delta_i, the local
component model keeps a_ik = grad_x ell_k dot delta_i. Let
S = sum_k exp(ell_k(root))/W. Its prediction is

    C_MS(i) = 1/W + S sum_k q_k exp(a_ik).

The matched scalar first-order model has only u=log((1-Base)/Base) and its
gradient. Its finite log-odds prediction collapses the component responses:

    C_scalar(i) = 1/W + S exp(sum_k q_k a_ik).

The gap is a finite-response Jensen gap, NOT independent information in Base,
not a physical bound on arbitrary hardware edits, and not a guarantee that the
local model remains accurate across network activation boundaries. A scalar
model with additional higher-order derivatives/evaluations can recover some of
this information; the comparison is at the stated first-order information and
candidate-query budgets. Our scalar control even receives per-stage/per-slice
unclipped Base and exact candidate W, beyond just the aggregate scalar CPI.

For two actions, approximate the full joint input displacement by delta_i+delta_j.
The component prediction retains the cross-response structure:

    C_MS(i+j)-C_MS(i)-C_MS(j)+C(root)
        = S sum_k q_k [exp(a_ik)-1][exp(a_jk)-1].

Opposing component responses can make this term NEGATIVE. Two finite single
moves can each be harmful, while their joint move is beneficial. The pair can
be scored from already computed single-action displacements and one root
component Jacobian, before a joint full-network query. The scalar collapse can
mis-rank the separate moves and miss the appropriate joint step sizes.
This is a concrete use of decomposition to propose/rank coupled resource
changes; it does not require assigning FP to front-end hardware or BP to a
specific resource. The parameter-to-component response is learned locally.

Implementation safeguards:
- Actual probability normalization, Base floor and CPI cap remain in the code.
- Candidate width W_min is exact. Paired pure resource moves keep W fixed.
- The chord is ONLY for pair proposal generation; selected proposals are
  fully re-encoded before final scalar/MSCS ranking.
- Both rankers receive the same legal pair universe and common proposed pool.
- Actual complete frozen-surrogate forward and exact McPAT area govern acceptance.
- All pressure-cycle explanations average alternative stage accounts; they do
  not sum four stages as disjoint elapsed cycles.

Evidence and limits as of 2026-10-11:
interaction_diamond_s42_01 is a POST-HOC frozen-surrogate witness on roms:
LQ40/IQ64:3.488019; LQ64/IQ64:3.500182; LQ40/IQ96:3.492131;
LQ64/IQ96:3.443889. Both single increases hurt, the pair helps by1.265%.
The automatic pair pilot then selected LQ64/IQ96 from693 legal pairs at this
state, without naming either resource in its selector. It beat a scalar ranker
choosing LQ72/IQ96; both had equal1-query budgets. It also replicated locally
at the next recorded roms state. These states were already inspected; this is
not independent confirmation, a complete new trajectory, or gem5 evidence.

This supports investigating Decouple through conditional joint response.
It does NOT establish the original Protect claim (small immediate reclaim
cost but lost future investment value). Protect still needs its own measured
conditional counterfactual. Large component norms alone were not validated.
