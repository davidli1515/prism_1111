Modified first-order Shepard interpolation, per slice:
  each center contributes its local linear log-excess-CPI or log-odds response;
  weights are inverse squared COMPLETE standardized input distance, normalized
  across centers. At an exact center, only zero-distance centers get weight.
  This is a convex combination of local affine responses, avoiding an ill-conditioned
  polynomial residual solve. Use at most the last six DISTINCT visited roots of
  THAT arm. A new center becomes available only after the arm has accepted it and
  computed its usual next-step root Jacobian. No gradients at rejected candidates,
  no new derivative queries, no access to another arm's or future roots.
  Root response and derivative are preserved (nonidentical centers), with scalar
  and MSCS getting the identical combination rule and own earlier derivatives.
  Scalar interpolates four per-slice log(CPI-1/W) responses and their true gradients;
  MSCS interpolates 4x3 log(P/Base) and their true gradients. Reconstruct CPI with
  existing physical clamps. One-center predictions use the original predictor
  exactly. No benchmark-specific rule, new threshold or length-scale tuning.
