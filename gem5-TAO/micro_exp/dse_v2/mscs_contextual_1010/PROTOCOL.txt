Conditional protect / decouple pilot, seed 42, fixed checkpoint, 2026-10-10

Question and connection to the paper
  The paper's Scancel and large response norm detect cancellation, but do not
  establish either useful complementarity or the future cost of reclamation.
  Here the proposed operational bridge is conditional resource value:
    I(u,v) = C(u+v) - C(u) - C(v) + C(root).
  Decouple: two increases have negative I and an improving joint endpoint.
  Protect: a decrease alone costs <0.3% CPI, but after a specified investment
  its cost exceeds 0.3%. The tolerance is inherited from refine_v3, not fitted.
  These are revised mechanisms, NOT a validation of Algorithm 2's original
  unconditional norm/max-cancellation rules. Avoid relabeling the prior gcc win.

Smallest test before a larger search
  Four roots: gcc and mcf first-quartile-area saved Pareto points excluding the
  preceding pilot's roots; xalan and leela middle-area saved Pareto points.
  Selection uses existing scalar CPI/area only, with the same checkpoint hash.
  One actual greedy decision per root and method. This is NOT a full trajectory
  or a statistically powered generalization result. No further expansion is automatic.
  Area cap = 1.10 * root area. Fixed widths, L1 trace size and BP type.
  Candidates: all legal one-stride edits in the previous 15-resource domain,
  plus all up/up and up/down edits of DIFFERENT parameters. No projection.
  Same exact candidate pool for every method; no MSCS-only candidate coverage.

Prediction and selection
  Rebuild COMPLETE normalized input for every actual joint endpoint. Do not
  approximate joint features by adding the two single endpoint displacements.
  Root Jacobians predict per-stage/slice CPI using the prior scalar-excess and
  component-logit rules. Independent scalar/MSCS tangents must agree.
  Methods: scalar, MSCS full, no_decouple, no_protect, no_context.
  Every method receives the same scalar anchor in its first verification slot.
  Its second slot is its highest-ranked distinct allowed candidate.
  Scalar uses its own nonlinear conditional-cost protection criterion too.
  Full MSCS uses its joint finite-component prediction for every endpoint;
  protected up/down actions are deferred only from its second proposal slot.
  No verified CPI improvement and no shared scalar anchor is vetoed by MSCS.
  no_decouple removes the predicted nonadditive term for up/up actions.
  no_protect removes it for up/down actions and disables protection deferral.
  no_context removes both; singles keep the identical MSCS finite-step predictions.
  Ablations expose dependence on joint response estimates; this does not prove
  that every joint estimate corresponds to causal architectural interaction.
  Rank by CPI, fixed pool-order ties. Evaluate exact CPI AND exact McPAT area of
  both proposals; accept best feasible strictly improving candidate. All proposals
  count even if infeasible. Every method has exactly two logical candidate calls.
  All methods' proposals are written and hashed before ANY candidate exact label.
  Identical frozen proposals may share physical evaluations, but budgets remain
  method-local. No wall-clock superiority claim from this shared work.

Bounded counterfactual audit
  Before querying labels, select <=1 decouple witness and <=1 protect witness
  per root. Prefer an MSCS-selected decouple pair; otherwise pick largest predicted
  negative interaction among improving up/up pairs. Protect witness is strongest
  predicted conditional-cost rise among flagged pairs with an improving investment.
  If no witness qualifies, record non-activation; never force one after seeing labels.
  AFTER all methods decide, evaluate each witness's two singles and joint endpoint,
  with exact areas. Thus check both predicted I and actual counterfactual I, and
  whether reclamation really saves area. These labels never return to selection.
  Max 40 logical search verifications + 24 audit verifications + 4 common roots.
  Physical query sharing is reported separately; root forward/backward costs too.
  No exhaustive truth labels for the joint pool; no hindsight optimum claim.

Success/failure reporting

  ablations. Report query, area, feature-generation, derivative and inference costs.
  For each witness report predicted and actual constituent effects, conditional
  costs, interaction sign, component changes and feasibility. A correctly flagged
  direction without accurate magnitude is not a calibrated predictor.
  The previous four roots are development evidence; these different four roots are
  exploratory confirmation only. No threshold adjustment or benchmark cherry-picking.
  If neither mechanism activates or validates, report this and do not scale the run.
  No new gem5 or retraining. All explanations remain frozen-surrogate counterfactuals.
