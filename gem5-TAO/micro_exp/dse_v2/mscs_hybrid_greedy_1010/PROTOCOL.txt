Multi-step scalar-anchor / MSCS-alternative greedy, seed 42, frozen checkpoint

Question
  Does retaining one scalar proposal protect against the observed MSCS
  over-conservatism while retaining its finite-step candidate-ranking benefit?
  This tests the proposed hybrid directly in trajectories, not joint-action lookahead.

Six preselected roots, no search based on outcomes
  Regression: gcc_middle and mcf_middle from mscs_jacobian_greedy_1010.
  Extension: all four roots of mscs_contextual_1010 (gcc rank3, mcf rank4,
  xalan rank6, leela rank7). These have already been inspected for one-step
  joint moves; they are NOT untouched held-out validation data.
  Report the two regression cases and four extensions separately.
  The checkpoint, source archives, normalizer and input selection stay unchanged.

Controlled comparison
  Arms: scalar_excess, mscs_logit, hybrid. All get the same generator and rules.
  Six rounds/root/arm, at most two previously unqueried candidates per round.
  Scalar: best two by scalar-excess prediction.
  MSCS: best two by component-logit prediction.
  Hybrid: scalar's best one, plus MSCS's best DISTINCT candidate at that same state.
  When they agree, the second MSCS candidate is used. No extra query is spent.
  Candidate identities are frozen before either exact CPI or area is evaluated.
  Evaluate both, accept the feasible strict CPI improvement with lowest CPI.
  Never accept an improvement solely on the predicted component attribution.
  Retaining scalar's first candidate does not guarantee dominance over scalar's
  two-candidate policy or over scalar's later trajectory.

Unchanged local search
  Exact action domain/step generator imported read-only from the original pilot:
  15 resource parameters, +/- strides1/2/4, no projection, fixed widths, L1 trace,
  BP type. Area cap = 1.10 * original root area.
  Accept -> radius4; reject both -> radius4/2/1. No parameter-specific adaptation.
  All tried configs are seen, including infeasible ones. No performance-label
  reuse between arms; exact area caches may be shared and must be source-salted.
  Pure methods use identical finite-step numerical models to the original pilot.
  Full feature endpoint displacement, not architecture-input slots alone.
  Hybrid reuses the scalar derivative recovered from the component Jacobian;
  initial slopes must match an independent scalar gradient and network JVP.
  Scalar requires 4 backward outputs/root; MSCS/hybrid 12. Record all compute costs.

Explanations and shadow information
  Every MSCS/hybrid trial logs its predicted and actual pressure-CPI changes.
  Record both scalar and MSCS predicted CPI plus the proposal's source.
  Protect/Decouple are not active selectors in this experiment. Single-action
  cancellation or resource shrinkage is not itself evidence of those mechanisms.
  No counterfactuals or hard protection vetoes are added. Their previous audit
  remains separate; do not retrofit a causal label onto a winning hybrid move.

Budget and preservation

  Root gradient forwards/JVP diagnostics counted/timed separately.
  No exhaustive initial-pool oracle audit, new gem5, retraining or follow-up sweep.
  All new sources/results confined to this folder. Original files/cache data are
